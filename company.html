Company involvment

Our COSI aims to foster Industry/Academia collaborations in the field of varianti-related research. In light of this effort, a few opportunities for companies to showcase products and services to the conference delegates hae been created. These 15-minute presentations are designed to create awareness of new technologies, services, etc (in a non-hard sales format). The cost for a Company Presentation is $1500. The cost does not include registration and the presenter must be registered to attend the conference. Requests for Company Presentations will be reviewed by the Organizing Committee. Space is limited and it may not be possible to accept all Company Presentation requests.

Sponsorship

We welcome company contributions to support the VarI-COSI activities. Non-presenting companies are welcome to purchase a supporting member registration at the level of $800. This registration includes: poster space in the COSI session, mention in the VarI-COSI program and ability to distribute company material to VarI-COSI participants.

Note: For information about the sponsorship of the VarI-COSI session and other activities of the ISMB conference please contact Seth Munholland.
